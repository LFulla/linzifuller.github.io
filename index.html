// about

Technology has always fascinated me — the systems, and the people who build them.

My passion for software began with games like Minecraft and League of Legends, sparking my curiosity about how software is built and optimized. In high school I competed in coding competitions, which led to a Lockheed Martin internship in 2018 — where I discovered an enthusiasm for engineering and leadership in equal measure.

I studied Computer Science and Mandarin at the University of Colorado Boulder, specializing in AI, ML, AR, VR, and automation. Though COVID-19 disrupted my degree, I stayed on at Lockheed Martin as a Software Engineer Intern, working on AR/VR applications before transitioning into Technical Project Management and leading college internship teams.

To formalize the leadership skills I'd been picking up on the job, I completed an 18-week Technical Project Management bootcamp at the University of Denver: Agile leadership, project lifecycles, cross-functional collaboration. I joined S&P Global as a Scrum Master, overseeing three software development teams on high-profile financial contracts. From there I became Engineering Manager at Returned.com, leading internal and outsourced teams in a fast-paced startup. After a stint at Convey as a Technical Project Manager delivering solutions for enterprise clients, I joined Accuris as Senior Agile Delivery Manager, running multiple cross-functional engineering teams to make sure we shipped what we promised.

Today I'm a Senior Software Engineer at Accuris on the Bridgeworks team, having recently switched from delivery leadership back into a hands-on engineering role. Across enterprise and startup, the through-line has been the same: solve complex problems, lead well, and deliver work I can be proud of.
